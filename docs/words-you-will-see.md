# Words you'll see

What the words in EasyJoey and in this guide mean, in plain language.
{ .meta }

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Encounter** | One visit. It holds everything from that day: the notes, prescriptions, orders and the bill. |
| **Active** | An encounter that is still open. You can still add to it. |
| **Ended** | An encounter that is finished. Its records are final. |
| **Chief complaint** | The main reason the patient came, in a few words. |
| **SOAP** | The four parts of a visit note: **S**ubjective (what the patient says), **O**bjective (what you measure and find), **A**ssessment (what you think it is) and **P**lan (what happens next). |
| **HPI** | History of the present illness: the story of the current problem. |
| **Vitals** | Blood pressure, pulse, breathing rate, temperature, oxygen level, height and weight. |
| **ICD-10** | The standard list of diagnosis codes, for example *J06.9* for an upper respiratory infection. PhilHealth and HMOs need the code. |
| **Coded diagnosis** | A diagnosis that has its ICD-10 code. |
| **Amend** | Correct a final record. The original stays in the record log. |
| **Entered in error** | Mark a final record as a mistake, for example one written for the wrong patient. It stays on the chart, struck through. |
| **Record log** | The history of a record: every change, who made it and when. |
| **Charges** | What the visit costs, taken from your price list. |
| **Invoice** | The bill. It gets its number when payment is collected. |
| **OR number** | The Official Receipt number printed on the receipt. |
| **Void** | Cancel a paid invoice, or some of its items, and refund the patient. |
| **Senior Citizen (SC) discount** | The 20% discount for patients aged 60 and over, with their OSCA ID. |
| **PWD discount** | The 20% discount for persons with disability, with their PWD ID. |
| **HMO** | A health plan company that pays part or all of a patient's bill. |
| **PhilHealth** | The government health insurance programme. |
| **Coverage** | The part of the bill an HMO or PhilHealth pays. |
| **LOA** | Letter of Authorization: the approval number an HMO gives for a visit. |
| **Accounts receivable** | Money that HMOs and PhilHealth still owe the clinic. |
| **Payment plan** | A big bill split into a down payment and monthly installments. |
| **Walk-in** | A patient who comes without an appointment. |
| **Check in** | Mark that a booked patient has arrived. It opens their encounter. |
| **No-show** | A booked patient who didn't come. |
| **Sync** | Copying changes between your clinic's iPads, and to EasyJoey Cloud if your plan has it. |
| **EasyJoey Cloud** | The part of your plan that keeps a copy online and lets you use EasyJoey in a web browser. |
| **LF Accounts** | The account page on the web where the clinic owner manages the clinic's plan, payment, team and iPads. |
| **Read-only** | You can open and read everything, but not change it. |
| **Archive** | Take a patient or an encounter off the list without deleting their records. |
