# Welcome to the EasyJoey guide

EasyJoey is a clinic app built for the iPad. Every patient is one folder: you see them, write their notes, bill and book in one place, even when the internet is down.
{ .lead }

This guide walks you through the everyday tasks, one page per task, with a picture of every step.

## What EasyJoey can't do yet

We'd rather tell you now than have you look for it:

- **Insurance claims** are tracked by hand. You mark a claim submitted or paid yourself. EasyJoey doesn't send claims to PhilHealth or HMOs.
- **Nothing is sent to patients.** Receipts open your iPad's Mail or Messages app, plan offers are only recorded, and appointment reminders go to your staff, not to patients.
- **Online booking requests** don't send staff a notification yet. Check **Awaiting confirmation** in **Appointments**.
- **Attachments** only shows files linked to a visit, such as a signed consent. You can't add, open or delete files there yet.
- **Consents** are recorded from a photo of the signed paper. Signing on the iPad screen isn't available yet.
- **Sharing records** works by printing. **Share PMD** isn't available yet.
- **Orders** aren't sent to a lab. Marking an order only records what happened.
- **Finished records stay final.** After an encounter ends, use **Amend…** or **Mark entered in error…** to correct a record. Only today's encounters can be reopened.
- **Issued invoices and payment plans** can't be edited. An invoice can be voided; a plan's terms can't be changed once its invoice is issued.
- **An archived patient** can't be brought back from the app yet.
- **Offline for up to 30 days.** After that the iPad turns read-only until it connects again. The **Network** page and backups are only on the iPad.

Something not working the way this guide says? Tell your EasyJoey contact, and mention the page and step number.
