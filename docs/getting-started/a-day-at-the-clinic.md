# A patient visit from start to finish

See how the pages of this guide fit together: one patient, from booking to payment. Each step links to the page with the full instructions.
{ .meta }

**Time:** about 3 minutes to read · **You'll need:** nothing. Read this before the other pages.
{ .meta }

## Before you start

- Every patient has a folder in EasyJoey. Everything about them goes in it: their visits, notes, prescriptions and bills.
- A visit is called an **encounter**. You start one when you see the patient, and end it when you're done.

## The visit, step by step

### 1. Book the patient (front desk)

The patient calls or books on your clinic's booking page. Tap **Appointments**, then **New appointment**, choose the patient, the reason and a free time, and tap **Book**.

A walk-in patient doesn't need a booking. Go straight to step 3.

More in [Appointments](../schedule/appointments.md).

### 2. Check the patient in (front desk)

When the patient arrives, open their appointment and tap **Check in**. EasyJoey opens today's encounter for them, with its bill ready.

More in [Appointments](../schedule/appointments.md).

### 3. Find the patient, or add a new one (front desk)

Search for the patient in **Patients** first. Add them only if they aren't there, so they never end up with two folders.

More in [Find or add a patient](../patients/find-or-add-a-patient.md).

### 4. Start the encounter (doctor or front desk)

If the patient was checked in, their encounter is already open. For a walk-in, open the patient and tap **Start encounter**.

More in [Start an encounter](../visits/start-an-encounter.md).

### 5. See the patient and write it down (doctor)

In the encounter, add the **Chief complaint**, the **Vitals**, your findings and the **Diagnosis**. Then write the **Prescription** and any lab or imaging **Orders**.

More in [Record notes in the encounter](../visits/record-notes.md) and [Prescriptions, orders and attachments](../visits/prescriptions-orders-attachments.md).

### 6. Check the charges, then end the encounter (doctor)

**Charges** at the top of the encounter shows what goes on the bill. The visit type is already there; add anything else you did. Then tap **End encounter**. The records become final and the bill moves to the Cashier.

More in [Finish the visit](../visits/finish-the-visit.md) and [Charges and the Cashier](../payments/charges-and-cashier.md).

### 7. Take payment (cashier)

Tap **Cashier**. The patient is on the **Waiting** list. Open their bill, apply a Senior Citizen, PWD or HMO discount if they have one, then tap **Collect** and give the receipt.

More in [Charges and the Cashier](../payments/charges-and-cashier.md).

### 8. Follow up later (owner or front desk)

Unpaid balances, HMO claims and payment plans are in **Billing**. How the clinic is doing is in **Analytics**.

More in [Billing, balances and payment plans](../payments/billing-and-payment-plans.md) and [Notifications and Analytics](../clinic/notifications-and-analytics.md).

## Tips

!!! tip "Set up these first"
    Before your first patient, fill in your clinic's details, opening hours, price list and payment methods. See [Settings basics](../clinic/settings-basics.md).

!!! info "Good to know"
    - Who does each step depends on your clinic. In a small clinic the doctor may do all of them.
    - Everything is saved on the iPad as you go, even without the internet. See [Syncing and working offline](../clinic/sync-and-offline.md).
    - New words? See [Words you'll see](../words-you-will-see.md).
